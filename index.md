  </div>
  <div class="is-flow">
    <div class="is-step"><b>Discover</b><span>Name the constraint and success criteria.</span></div>
    <div class="is-step"><b>Select</b><span>Pick the lab, module, provider, and validation level.</span></div>
    <div class="is-step"><b>Deploy</b><span>Apply Terraform and Kubernetes patterns safely.</span></div>
    <div class="is-step"><b>Validate</b><span>Record what is local, cloud-tested, or customer-specific.</span></div>
    <div class="is-step"><b>Handoff</b><span>Turn the work into runbooks, training, and support paths.</span></div>
