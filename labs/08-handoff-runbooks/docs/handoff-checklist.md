## Handoff Philosophy

**Goal:** Ensure smooth transition from implementation to operations, leaving customer team feeling empowered and confident.

**Principles:**
- Complete transparency
- Comprehensive documentation
- Thorough training
- Ongoing support
- Customer empowerment
