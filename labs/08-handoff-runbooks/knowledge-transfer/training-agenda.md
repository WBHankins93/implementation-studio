**Goal:** Empower the customer team to operate the system independently with confidence.

**Principles:**

- Real-world scenarios
- Gradual knowledge transfer
- Documentation for reference
- Ongoing support availability

## Success Criteria

Training is successful when:

- [ ] Customer team can access and navigate the system
- [ ] Customer team can monitor system health
- [ ] Customer team can perform basic operations
- [ ] Customer team can troubleshoot common issues
- [ ] Customer team knows when to escalate
- [ ] Customer team feels confident and empowered
