# Certification Checklist

**Application:** [Application Name]  
**Purpose:** Validate customer team operational readiness

## Certification Philosophy

**Goal:** Ensure customer team is confident and capable of operating the system independently.

**Approach:**
- Practical demonstration over written tests
- Real-world scenarios
- Gradual skill validation
- Ongoing support available

## Prerequisites

Before certification, customer team should have:

- [ ] Completed all training sessions
- [ ] Completed all hands-on exercises
- [ ] Reviewed all documentation
- [ ] Had opportunity to ask questions

## Certification Assessment

- Scenario-based questions
- Short answer questions

**Scenarios:**
1. Deploy new version

## Certification Levels

### Level 1: Basic Operations

**Requirements:**
- Can perform routine operations
- Can monitor system
- Can follow runbooks
- Knows when to escalate

**Certification:**
- Complete all exercises
- Pass practical assessment
- Demonstrate basic competency

### Level 2: Advanced Operations

**Requirements:**
- Can troubleshoot issues
- Can perform complex operations
- Can make operational decisions
- Can train others

**Certification:**
- Complete Level 1
- Pass advanced assessment
- Demonstrate advanced competency
- Complete training others

### Level 3: Expert Operations

**Requirements:**
- Can handle all operations
- Can design solutions
- Can optimize system
- Can lead operations

**Certification:**
- Complete Level 2
- Pass expert assessment
- Demonstrate expert competency
- Complete leadership training

## Certification Process

### Step 1: Self-Assessment

Customer team completes self-assessment:
- Review competencies
- Identify strengths
- Identify areas for improvement
- Request additional training if needed

### Step 2: Practical Assessment

Trainer conducts practical assessment:
- Observe operations
- Evaluate skills
- Provide feedback
- Identify gaps

### Step 3: Certification Decision

**Certified:**
- All core competencies demonstrated
- Can operate independently
- Knows when to escalate
- Has access to support

**Conditional Certification:**
- Most competencies demonstrated
- Minor gaps identified
- Additional training scheduled
- Support available

**Not Certified:**
- Significant gaps identified
- Additional training required
- Re-assessment scheduled
- Support available

### Step 4: Certification Documentation

**Document:**
- Certification level
- Competencies validated
- Areas for improvement
- Ongoing support plan

## Post-Certification

### Immediate Support (First 30 Days)

- **Availability:** Business hours + on-call
- **Response Time:** < 2 hours
- **Support Channels:** Email, Slack, Phone

### Ongoing Support

- **Availability:** Business hours
- **Response Time:** < 24 hours
- **Support Channels:** Email, Ticket system

### Continuous Improvement

- **Regular Check-ins:** Monthly for first 3 months
- **Feedback Collection:** Ongoing
- **Additional Training:** As needed
- **Documentation Updates:** As system evolves

## Re-Certification

### When Required

- Major system changes
- New features added
- Significant time elapsed (annually)
- Team member changes

### Process

- Review current competencies
- Identify new requirements
- Complete additional training
- Re-assess skills
- Update certification

## Success Criteria

Certification is successful when:

- [ ] Customer team can operate system independently
- [ ] Customer team feels confident
- [ ] Customer team knows when to escalate
- [ ] Customer team has all necessary tools
- [ ] Customer team has ongoing support
- [ ] Customer feels empowered

---

**Remember:** Certification is not a test—it's validation of readiness and empowerment.
